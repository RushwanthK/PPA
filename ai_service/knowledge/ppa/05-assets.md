# Assets (investments) in PPA

The **Assets** page tracks investments and other things you own that have a money value.

## Adding an asset

Enter:

- **Name:** must be unique, for example "Nifty 50 index fund".
- **Platform** (optional): where it is held, for example a broker or fund house.
- **Category:** one of:
  - Provident Fund
  - Mutual Funds
  - Stocks
  - ETF
  - FD (fixed deposit)
  - Other
- **Balance:** its current value.

The category decides how the asset appears in the **asset allocation** chart on the dashboard.

## Deposits and withdrawals

- **Deposit:** you added money, for example a new SIP instalment or more units. The asset's balance goes up.
- **Withdraw:** you took money out or sold part of it. The balance goes down and cannot go below zero.

Asset transactions are **not linked to a bank** in PPA. If you paid for an investment from your bank account, record the bank expense separately if you want the bank balance to match.

## Keeping values current

Market-linked investments (stocks, mutual funds, ETFs) change value every day. PPA stores the value you record. It does not fetch live prices. Update balances regularly, for example monthly, so your net worth stays realistic.

## History, downloads and deleting

Each asset has a transaction history that can be filtered and downloaded. When deleting an asset, PPA offers to back up its transactions first.

## Deleting an asset

An asset can be deleted only when its **balance is zero**.

1. On the **Assets** page, find the asset in the list.
2. If its balance is not zero, first record a **Withdraw** transaction for the remaining amount (**Add Transaction**, type **Withdraw**), so the balance becomes Rs. 0. While the balance is not zero, the delete dialog explains that the asset cannot be deleted.
3. Click the asset's **Delete** button. The **Delete Asset** dialog opens and shows the current balance.
4. Optionally, under **Backup Asset Transactions**, download the asset's transaction history as **Excel** or **PDF**.
5. Click **Delete Asset** (or **Delete Without Backup** if you skipped the backup).

Deleting an asset also deletes its transaction history and cannot be undone, so download the backup if you may need the records. Your net worth and the dashboard's asset allocation update straight away.

Assets are separate from bank and savings accounts: deleting an asset does not change any bank or savings balance.
