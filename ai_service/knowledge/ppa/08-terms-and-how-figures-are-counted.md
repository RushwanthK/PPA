# PPA terms, and how the assistant counts your figures

## Transaction types in PPA

- **Expense:** money going out of a bank account or card: something you bought, a bill you paid, money you sent to someone.
- **Income:** money coming into a bank account: salary, interest, a refund, money someone sent you.
- **Payment (credit cards):** paying your credit card bill. It reduces what you owe on the card; it is not new spending, because the purchases were already recorded on the card.
- **Deposit (savings):** money you put into a savings account.
- **Withdrawal (savings):** money you take out of a savings account.

## Credit card figures

- **Limit:** the most you can owe on the card.
- **Used:** what you have spent on the card and not yet paid.
- **Available limit:** limit minus used.
- **Billed (unpaid):** purchases already on a statement that you still have to pay.
- **Unbilled spends:** purchases in the current billing cycle, not yet on a statement.
- **Total payable:** what you owe in all.

## Dashboard figures

- **Net worth:** bank balances plus savings plus assets, minus credit card debt.
- **Asset allocation:** how your assets are split across categories (for example stocks, mutual funds, gold).

## How the assistant counts "My data" answers

When you ask about your own transactions ("how much did I spend on food in March?"):

- **Spending counts money going out, and income counts money coming in.** The two are never added together.
- **Money movement is left out of totals** when you ask for overall spending or income without search words: transfers between your own accounts, card bill payments, investments and similar categories. Moving money between your own accounts is not spending. The answer says how many such transactions were left out.
- **When you search for a name, place or merchant** ("Goa trip", "Zomato"), those transactions are kept, so payments to friends for a trip count. Ask to leave out transfers if you want them excluded.
- **Savings accounts:** a withdrawal counts as money going out. Deposits are money you moved into savings, not income, so they are left out of totals. Savings transactions filed as transfers, card bills or investments are always left out.
- **Money moved between your own accounts may be counted twice** if it was entered on both sides (for example cash entered as bank income and also as a savings deposit). The answer reminds you when savings transactions are included.
- **The search reads descriptions and categories.** A transaction whose description does not mention the trip, person or merchant you ask about cannot be found by that name.
- **Figures are calculated by PPA, not by the AI model.** The tables under each answer show the transactions that were counted, so you can check them.

## Two modes

- **Help** (default): answers questions about how PPA works and general money topics from PPA's help articles.
- **My data:** answers questions about your own accounts and transactions.
