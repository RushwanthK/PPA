# Savings accounts in PPA

A savings account in PPA is money you set aside from a bank account, for example an emergency fund or a goal such as a trip. Every savings account is **linked to one bank**.

## Adding a savings account

Click **Add Savings Account** and choose:

- **Account name:** must be unique.
- **Bank:** the bank it is linked to. This is required, and the bank must exist.

You can later move a savings account to a different bank with **Edit Savings Account**. It must always stay linked to some bank.

## Deposits and withdrawals

Savings transactions move money between the savings account and its linked bank:

| Type | Savings balance | Linked bank balance |
|---|---|---|
| **Deposit** | goes up | goes down by the same amount |
| **Withdrawal** | goes down | goes up by the same amount |

Rules:

- The amount must be greater than zero.
- A deposit needs enough money in the linked bank, otherwise you see "Insufficient bank balance".
- A withdrawal needs enough money in the savings account, otherwise you see "Insufficient balance".

Because money only moves between your own accounts, a deposit into savings does **not** count as spending and does not change your net worth.

## History and downloads

Each savings account has its own transaction history, with filters for type (deposit or withdrawal), dates and search. It can be downloaded as Excel or PDF.

## Deleting a savings account

A savings account can be deleted only when its balance is zero. Withdraw the remaining money back to the bank first. PPA offers **Backup Savings Transactions** before deleting.
