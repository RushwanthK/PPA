# Bank accounts in PPA

The **Banks** page lists your bank accounts with their current balances.

## Adding a bank

Click **Add Bank** and enter:

- **Bank name:** must be unique across your accounts.
- **Opening balance:** the amount currently in the account. It cannot be negative.

## Editing a bank

Use **Edit Bank** to rename it. The balance is not edited directly. It changes only through transactions, so the history always explains the balance.

## Recording transactions

Each bank transaction is either:

- **Income:** money coming in, such as salary, refunds or interest. The balance goes up.
- **Expense:** money going out, such as rent, bills or shopping. The balance goes down.

Rules:

- The amount must be greater than zero. The type (income or expense) decides the direction.
- An expense cannot be larger than the current balance. PPA shows "Insufficient balance" instead of letting the account go negative.
- Add a **category** (for example Food, Rent, Travel) and a **description**. Categories feed the spending chart on the dashboard.

Every transaction stores the **balance after** it was applied, so you can follow how the balance changed over time.

## Viewing and filtering history

Open a bank's transactions to see its history. You can filter by type (income or expense), date range and amount range, and search by description or category. Long histories load page by page.

## Downloading transactions

Transactions can be downloaded as **Excel** or **PDF** for your records or for sharing with an accountant.

## Deleting a bank

A bank can be deleted only when:

1. its balance is exactly zero, and
2. no savings account is linked to it.

Before deleting, PPA offers **Backup Bank Transactions** so you can download the history first. **Delete Without Backup** skips that step.

## Money moving to savings

Deposits into a linked savings account take money out of the bank, and withdrawals from savings put it back. See "Savings accounts in PPA".
