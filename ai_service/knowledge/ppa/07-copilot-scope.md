# What the PPA assistant can and cannot do

The PPA assistant answers questions in two ways.

## Questions about PPA and general money topics

For example: "How do I record a credit card payment?" or "What is an emergency fund?". The assistant looks up PPA's help articles and its financial-education notes, answers from them, and shows which articles it used. If the articles do not cover the question, it says so instead of guessing.

## Questions about your own accounts

For example: "What is my total bank balance?" or "How did my spending change over the last three months?". The assistant reads your data through the same secure PPA API that the app uses, with your own login. It can only see your accounts. The figures are calculated by PPA, not estimated by the AI model.

## What it will not do

- **No personalised investment advice.** It will not tell you which stock, fund or product to buy or sell. It can explain how things work so that you can decide, or suggest speaking to a registered adviser.
- **No changes to your data.** The assistant only reads. Adding, editing or deleting accounts and transactions is done in the app itself.
- **No access to other users' data.**
- **No topics unrelated to PPA or personal finance.**

## Usage limits

Each user has a daily limit on assistant questions, so the service stays available and within its budget.
