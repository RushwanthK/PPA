# The PPA dashboard

The **Dashboard** combines all of your accounts into one view.

## Summary cards

| Figure | How PPA calculates it |
|---|---|
| **Total bank balance** | Sum of all your bank balances |
| **Total savings** | Sum of all your savings account balances |
| **Total assets** | Sum of all your asset balances |
| **Credit card debt** | Sum of what you owe on all cards |
| **Net worth** | Assets + bank balances + savings − credit card debt |

Net worth is the single best number for tracking progress over time. It goes up when you save or investments grow, and down when debt grows.

Moving money between your own accounts, such as from a bank into savings, does not change net worth. The money only changes place.

## Spending by category

This chart groups your **expenses** by category. It includes:

- bank expenses, and
- credit card expenses.

It does not include income, savings deposits, asset deposits or credit card payments. Paying a card bill is not new spending, because the spending was already counted when you used the card.

Time ranges:

- last 30 days
- 3 months
- 6 months
- 1 year
- all time

Transactions without a category appear as uncategorized. Adding categories makes the chart more useful.

## Asset allocation

This chart shows how your assets are split across categories (Provident Fund, Mutual Funds, Stocks, ETF, FD, Other). Use it to check that you are not depending too heavily on one type of investment.

## Freshness

Dashboard figures update after you add or change transactions. If a number looks out of date right after a change, refresh the page.
