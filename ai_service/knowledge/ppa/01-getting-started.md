# Getting started with PPA

PPA (Personal Portfolio App) is a personal finance tracker. It keeps all of your money in one place: bank accounts, savings accounts, credit cards and investments (assets). The dashboard combines them into a single view of your net worth and spending. Amounts are shown in Indian rupees (₹).

## Creating an account and logging in

Register with a unique user name, a password, and your age, date of birth and place. After registering, log in with your user name and password.

If you enter a wrong password several times in a row, PPA asks you to wait a few minutes before trying again. This protects your account from password-guessing attacks.

A login lasts for one day. After that you are asked to log in again.

## What you can track

- **Banks:** current or salary accounts, with income and expense transactions.
- **Savings:** savings accounts that are always linked to one of your banks. Money moves between the bank and the savings account.
- **Credit cards:** spending, payments, billed and unbilled amounts, and available limit.
- **Assets:** investments such as mutual funds, stocks, ETFs, fixed deposits and provident fund.
- **Dashboard:** net worth, spending by category, and asset allocation.

## Your data is private

Every bank, card, asset and transaction belongs to the user who created it. You can only see and change your own data. Another user cannot read your accounts, even if they know an account number in the app.

## Profile, password and logging out

Open the **Profile** dialog to update your details.

- **Changing your password** requires your current password. After a password change, PPA logs you out on all other devices, so anyone who knew the old password loses access.
- **Log out** ends the session on the current device only.
- **Log out of all devices** ends every active session for your account, for example if you used PPA on a shared or lost device.

## Deleting your account

Your profile can be deleted from the Profile dialog. PPA first checks whether anything still needs your attention, such as balances that are not zero, and tells you what to clear before deleting.
